Roofing, Solar & Windows: What to Know Before You Compare

Roofing, solar, window and HVAC projects are major investments. Getting more than one estimate helps you compare scope, materials, timelines and price before you commit.

What can affect your options

  • The size and complexity of the project
  • Materials and brands used
  • Permits, labor and timeline
  • Warranty terms for the work and the products

Questions worth asking a provider

  • Verify the contractor's license, insurance and references
  • Get written estimates that list materials and scope
  • Never pay the full amount upfront
