Final Expense Insurance: What to Know Before You Compare

Final expense insurance is a small life insurance policy often used to help cover funeral costs, medical bills and other end-of-life expenses. Policies differ in price, waiting periods and how quickly benefits are paid.

What can affect your options

  • Your age and general health
  • The coverage amount you choose
  • Tobacco use
  • Whether the policy has a waiting period or graded benefit

Questions worth asking a provider

  • Ask whether the full benefit is paid from day one or phased in over time
  • Ask whether premiums can change
  • Compare total cost over time, not only the monthly premium
