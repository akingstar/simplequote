Home Insurance: What to Know Before You Compare

Home insurance usually combines protection for the structure, your belongings and liability. Policies differ in what they cover and how claims are paid, so comparing is worthwhile.

What can affect your options

  • Your home's age, size, construction and roof condition
  • Location, including local weather risks and claims history
  • Dwelling and personal property coverage limits
  • Deductible type, such as a flat amount or a percentage

Questions worth asking a provider

  • Ask whether the policy pays replacement cost or actual cash value
  • Ask what is excluded; flood and earthquake damage often need separate coverage
  • Check liability limits and any special limits on valuables
