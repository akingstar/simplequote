Overview

Health Insurance: What to Know Before You Compare

Health coverage comes in several forms, including employer plans, marketplace plans, short-term plans and Medicare-related options. Eligibility and enrollment periods differ, so a licensed provider can explain what applies to you.

What can affect your options

  • Your age, location and household size
  • Whether you qualify for financial assistance or a special enrollment period
  • Plan type and the provider network
  • Premium, deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

Questions worth asking a provider

  • Check that your doctors and prescriptions are covered
  • Compare total yearly cost, not just the monthly premium
  • Ask whether a plan is short-term; these often have fewer protections
