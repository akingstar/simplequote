Pest Control: What to Know Before You Compare

Pest problems range from a few ants to a termite infestation, and the right treatment depends on the pest and the property. Comparing local providers helps you understand your options and typical service plans.

What can affect your options

  • The type of pest and how widespread it is
  • Property size and type
  • One-time treatment versus a recurring plan
  • Follow-up visits and any service guarantee

Questions worth asking a provider

  • Ask whether the provider is licensed in your state
  • Ask what products are used, especially around children and pets
  • Ask what happens if the pests come back
