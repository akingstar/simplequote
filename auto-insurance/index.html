Auto Insurance: What to Know Before You Compare

Auto insurance prices differ from one provider to the next, even for the same driver. Requesting several quotes is one of the simplest ways to see the range of options available to you.

What can affect your options

  • Your driving record and claims history
  • The vehicle's make, model, age and safety features
  • Where the car is kept (ZIP code) and how far you drive
  • Coverage limits, deductibles and optional add-ons

Questions worth asking a provider

  • Ask what each quote includes: liability limits, collision and comprehensive
  • Compare the deductible, not only the monthly price
  • Ask about discounts such as multi-policy or safe driver
